Methodology

Stage 1 ranks the full universe on price data alone (liquidity, trend, HV range, RSI) and forwards the top 105 to Stage 2, which pulls the 25–50 DTE option chain, fundamentals and earnings dates. Each strategy applies hard filters (liquidity at the target strike, earnings exclusion, trend direction) and then scores 0–100 across weighted factors shown under each score. IV Rank uses the stored ATM-IV history once ≥60 days exist; before that a realised-vol distribution proxy is used and flagged. Deltas are Black–Scholes from chain IV. Not investment advice.
